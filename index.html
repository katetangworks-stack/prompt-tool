空間錯視 Prompt▾

不是炸裂特效，是讓人眼覺得空間怪。

Analyze the original photo's spatial structure first.
Identify compositional flow, illusion anchor, and possible depth reassignment.
Create a subtle spatial contradiction using local spatial displacement and depth-plane inconsistency.
Keep the subject believable.
Avoid generic breakout, giant hands, giant feet, fisheye, picture frame, superhero pose, surreal redesign, liquid melting, and AI fantasy environment.
Add only minimal cinematic enhancement.
Goal: viewer feels the space is slightly wrong, not that the effect is explosive.

局部深度重分配▾

只改局部空間，不破壞照片可信度。

Create a subtle local depth reassignment.
Source image context: [describe image]
Keep identity, lighting direction, lens feeling, and original environment.
Use a small spatial offset along the original compositional flow.
Create depth-plane inconsistency only around [anchor object].
Avoid dramatic fantasy, fisheye, broken frame, giant limbs, and explosive effects.
